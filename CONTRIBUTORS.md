# Contributors

Thank you to all the people who have contributed to Sage.is projects!

## How to Become a Contributor

We welcome contributions from everyone! To become a contributor:

1. Read our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md)
2. Review our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
3. Make your first contribution
4. Your name will be added here automatically or manually

### How to Add Yourself

If you've contributed to the project and don't see your name here, please feel free to add yourself by:

1. Opening a pull request
2. Adding your information in the appropriate section
3. Following the existing format

### Format

```markdown
- [Your Name](https://github.com/yourusername) - Brief description of contribution
```
